In July 2026, the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (CGEB) will replace the GST/HST credit. Under the new program name, there will be increased amounts, but the eligibility, payment calculation, and structure will be same as the GST/HST credit. Quarterly payment amounts will increase by 25% for 5 years (from 2026 to 2031) starting in July 2026.

As a part of the transition to the new CGEB, an additional one-time top-up payment will be provided to individuals who were entitled to receive the GST/HST credit in January 2026. This immediate support will be issued no later than June 2026.

These measures will provide increased financial support to more than 12 million Canadians with low and modest incomes to help manage the costs of everyday essentials.
